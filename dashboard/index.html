Model & validation

Size curve first. Among the protocols in this snapshot that already launched a token, market cap follows TVL along a power law. radar-4 fits that curve by ordinary least squares on log–log axes: value = eα · TVLβ, using only the snapshot's own figures — nothing is hardcoded and no live prices are fetched.

Category relative factor. Each category trades above or below the size curve. The factor is the median ratio of actual to curve-fitted market cap among the category's launched peers, shrunk toward 1.0 (prior weight 6) and clamped to [0.30, 2.50], so thin categories cannot dominate the board.

Adjustments. The base value is then scaled by 7-day TVL growth (clamped, weight 0.35), by maturity (a protocol listed longer earns up to the full factor over three years), and by chain breadth (up to +15% for five extra chains). The band around each value is the model's own residual spread on its peers: one standard deviation of log residuals, applied symmetrically.

Holdout honesty check. The peer set is split deterministically (sorted by slug; even positions fit, odd positions are predicted). The table shows median absolute log error (MedALE — lower is better) and the share of predictions landing within a factor of two of the actual market cap, for radar-4 against the previous radar-3 style category multiples and a single flat multiple.
