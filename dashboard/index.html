Launch Radar

Pre-token protocols, valued from their own record — model radar-5, refitted in this browser's data file

Implied value = value if it launches × odds it launches. The value is a size curve fitted on the snapshot's launched peers; every adjustment term had to earn its place out-of-sample (below) or it was dropped. Odds are the share of comparable peers that record a token, by age and size. Young protocols are censored: no token yet is not never.

Farm scenario

Verdicts recompute live. Default: 7.5% of the launch value goes to farmers; ticket = 0.02% of TVL, clamped to $250–$10,000. FARM needs a positive net that also pays ≥ $15/hour of the plan's time (a T-bill pays ~4% for none); MARGINAL is a positive net below that hurdle; SKIP loses money. Verdict stability counts how often the default verdict survives a 3×3 grid of drop shares and ticket scales.

Eligibility, stated once and applied to all: a protocol is ranked only when the snapshot records no token for it (no market cap, no symbol, no Gecko id) and it is not a bridge, liquid-staking, restaking or exchange product, not a versioned re-listing, and not a wrapped or staked product of an asset that already trades. Everything else is set aside in the accounting above, never silently dropped.
